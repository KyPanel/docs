title: Webmail and mail tools

---

Every mailbox has webmail (Roundcube) at `https://webmail.YOUR-DOMAIN/` (or `https://SERVER:7712/`). Sign in with the full email address and the mailbox password. The account owner opens any mailbox from **Email Accounts › Check Email** without the password.

## Mail tools

Mailbox users manage their own mailbox from the webmail's **Settings › Mail tools**. Each tool opens in a new tab, signed in to that mailbox only (the account owner does the same in the customer panel):

- **Password:** change the mailbox password;
- **Filters:** sort, forward or refuse incoming mail with rules;
- **Autoresponder:** an automatic reply while away, with start and end dates;
- **Email apps:** settings for Outlook, Apple Mail, Thunderbird and phones.

## Phones and desktop apps

See [Configure Mail Client](/user/email/#configure-mail-client). Most apps set themselves up from the email address alone.
